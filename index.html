Welcome to Nachiyar

Your family's trusted savings partner

Nachiyar Chit Fund Private Limited is a registered company based in Vellore. Since 2018 we have helped families, traders and salaried people save regularly and get a lump sum when they need it.

Every chit runs through an open auction. The auction discount is shared with all members as dividend, and our commission is a fixed 5%. Everything is simple and transparent.

  • Live oral auction, no fixed allotment
  • Full details and calculations on our mobile app
  • Quick processing and secure transactions
