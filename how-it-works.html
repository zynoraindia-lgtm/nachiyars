How It Works

Step by step

The complete chit journey

1

Choose your plan

Pick a plan with an instalment and duration you can pay comfortably. Call us if you need help choosing.

2

Join with your documents

Give your Aadhaar, PAN, photo and address proof to join a group. Everyone in a group pays the same amount for the same period.

3

Pay your instalment

Pay every month (every week in Honey Weekly, every day in Super Jet). You get a receipt for every payment.

4

Live oral auction

Each period has one auction. Members who need money call out the discount they will give. The highest discount wins the chit.

5

Winner gets the money

The winner receives the chit amount minus the discount, released quickly after surety documents are checked.

6

Dividend for everyone

After the 5% commission, the rest of the discount is shared equally by all members. Your next instalment is lower by that amount.

7

Everyone receives once

By the last instalment, every member has received the chit once. Those who wait longer gain the most.

Auction rules

Fair and transparent auctions

Oral auction only

Open bidding in front of all members.

No fixed allotment

No one is given the chit in advance.

5% formal commission

Only a fixed, declared commission.

Bid up to 30%

In Honey Weekly and Super Jet schemes.
