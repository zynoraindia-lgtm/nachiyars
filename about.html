About Us

Our story

Building trust, growing together

Nachiyar began in Vellore in 2018 with a simple belief: ordinary families and traders should be able to meet big needs without the burden of loans. The best way to do that is the traditional chit system, run honestly and properly.

Today, as Nachiyar Chit Fund Private Limited, a registered company, we run chits from ₹25,000 to ₹1 crore. Daily, weekly and monthly plans, live auctions, a mobile app and a 15-day interest-free facility are all there for our members' convenience.

Like the name we carry, we protect every member's money with the care we would give our own family's savings.
